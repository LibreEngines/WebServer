Licensing & Usage

Open Source License

All LibreGrad Engines designs and documentation are released under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0) license. Our software tools use the MIT License.

  • Use designs for personal or commercial projects
  • Modify and improve our designs freely
  • Share your modifications with the community
  • Attribute LibreGrad Engines in derivative works
  • Build and sell engines based on our designs

Philosophy

We believe that engineering knowledge should be accessible to everyone. By open sourcing our designs, we hope to inspire the next generation of builders, foster innovation, and create a community where ideas can be freely shared and improved upon.

Whether you're a student learning about engines, a hobbyist building a custom project, or a professional developing new products, our designs are here for you to use, learn from, and build upon.
